Do frequent numbers have a better chance of winning?

No. Frequency counts describe previous draws. In a fair random draw, past appearances do not make a valid combination more likely to win. Generated combinations are not predictions or tickets.

Why can frequency counts differ from another source?

Sources can use different date ranges or game-rule periods. Refresh downloads the available dataset; it does not guarantee that the latest draw has been included. Verify results and current rules with the official lottery operator.

Where are my saved combinations?

Saved sets and favorites stay locally on your device. There is no app account or account-based recovery service. Keep any shared copies you need before deleting the app or changing devices; device-backup behavior depends on your Apple settings.

How do I change notification or tracking permission?

Open iOS Settings to manage Lottery Numbers notifications. Tracking permission is under Privacy & Security → Tracking. Turning notifications off does not automatically delete an existing PushCore installation record.

Why is an upgrade or restore button unavailable?

An upgrade can be unavailable if the store offer cannot be loaded. Check your internet connection and try again. A disabled button does not charge your account. To recover a previous Pro purchase, use Restore purchases in Settings or on the upgrade screen with the Apple Account used to buy it. Pro is a one-time purchase, not a recurring subscription. Contact us if your purchase still does not unlock Pro.

How do I delete data or make a privacy request?

Delete individual saved sets from the Saved screen. Shared copies, backups, and server records are separate. For information held by our services, contact support@betscore.app; please do not send passwords, tokens, or installation credentials.
